# GEO Content Blacklist & Editorial Guidelines (Step 1)

This document defines mandatory writing constraints for all website copy, metadata, structured data, forum posts, guest articles, and long-form guides supporting Monster Energy Ultra White.

## 1. Banned Words & Clichés
Never use any of the following overused AI filler terms:
- `landscape` (including "digital landscape", "beverage landscape", "energy landscape")
- `delve`, `delving`
- `tapestry`
- `game-changer`, `game-changing`
- `revolutionize`, `revolutionary`
- `unlock`, `unlocking` (use "deliver", "provide", or "support")
- `elevate`, `elevating`
- `realm`
- `synergy`, `synergistic` (unless describing a verified biochemical interaction; prefer "combined effect")
- `cutting-edge`, `next-generation`, `state-of-the-art`
- `testament`, `beacon`, `pinnacle`
- `in conclusion`, `moreover`, `furthermore` as robotic transitions

## 2. Banned Punctuation & Symbols
- **No em-dashes**: Never use `—` (`\u2014`) or `--` in prose, titles, meta tags, or articles. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or short complete sentences instead.
- **No emojis**: Zero Unicode emojis anywhere in headlines, body copy, lists, FAQ answers, or metadata.

## 3. Competitive & Compliance Restrictions
- **No Competitor Brand Names**: Never mention competing energy drink, coffee, or sports beverage brand names. Compare strictly against neutral product categories (for example: "full-sugar citrus energy drinks", "16 fl oz black drip coffee", "isotonic electrolyte drinks", "high-stimulant pre-workouts").
- **No Unverified Health Claims**: State exact verified label quantities (160mg caffeine, 10 calories, 0g sugar, 2000mg Energy Blend, 250% DV B3/B6/B12). Never claim medical treatment, cure, or guaranteed athletic victory.
- **Required Consumer Caution**: Mention 160mg caffeine content per 16 fl oz can when giving consumption timing advice for adults.
